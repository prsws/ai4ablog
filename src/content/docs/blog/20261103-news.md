**The Reasoning Arm: a proposed design, in progress.** Until now the Reasoning Arm
was a boundary and one thing built: an inference endpoint on the Mac Studio. We
have drafted a design for what it could become, a thinker that plans, analyzes and
explains but is structurally unable to act. **Nothing in it is settled.** The
[Introduction](/arms/reasoning/introduction/) says what it proposes, where it
departs from the earlier page, and where it does not yet cover it. The full
[Requirements & Feature List](/arms/reasoning/reasoning-urs/) is the ledger the
design will evolve in, with each change dated and its reason recorded.

**Help wanted.** We would like people to try to break this design before any of it
is adopted, using the tools they prefer, and tell us what they find. The questions
and how to send answers are in the
[Call for Review](/arms/reasoning/pra-call-for-review/).

**The earlier page is deprecated, not removed.**
[The Reasoning Arm](/arms/reasoning/the-reasoning-arm/) is kept as written and now
points to the new pages.
