### What We Collect

AI4Aging.org does not collect personal information from visitors. We do not use tracking pixels, advertising networks, or deep analytics platforms. The one exception is anything you choose to send us yourself — see [Things You Send Us](#things-you-send-us).

To get this site found on the internet we submitted it to Google Search Console and it left a TXT record on our DNS. We don't know what kind of data collection that does and currently have neither the inclination nor the time to find out as we're busy. You are hereby notified that Google will do its thing when you visit this site.

### Things You Send Us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you put in the message.

**"Oye Pepa!" voice recordings.** If you choose to email a recording to oyepepa@ai4aging.org, we keep the audio and your email address. The audio is identified by a random ID, not your name. Your email address is stored separately and used only for withdrawals, for credit if you ask for it, and for required notices. You are named publicly only if you ask to be credited. Recordings are not posted publicly and are shared only with recipients who have agreed to the terms. You can withdraw a recording at any time by replying "retirar" or "withdraw" to your original email; it is removed within 30 days.

These recordings are governed by the [Oye Pepa! Voice Corpus Terms](/general/oyepepa-terms-en/) ([en español](/general/oyepepa-terms-es/)). Where this page and the terms differ for a recording, the terms apply.

### Hosting

This site is hosted on **GitHub Pages**. Like any web host, GitHub may log visitor information — such as your IP address — for security and operational purposes. Those logs belong to GitHub, are governed by the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement), and we never see them. We ourselves receive, store, and analyze nothing about your visit.

### Cookies

This site does not use cookies for tracking or analytics. Your browser may cache static assets (images, scripts, fonts) as part of normal web operation. Fonts are bundled with the site itself — nothing is fetched from third-party font services when you visit.

### Third-Party Services

Except for GitHub Pages hosting, the Google Search Console submission, and the YouTube videos described below, no third-party analytics, advertising, or data brokers are integrated.

**YouTube.** Two pages embed YouTube videos: [Pepa in Action](/media/overview/) and [5min PowerPoint](/general/5minpowerpoint/). When either page opens, your browser fetches the video's preview image from Google's servers (`i.ytimg.com`), which means Google receives your IP address along with that request. If you press play, the page loads YouTube's player from `youtube-nocookie.com`, and Google's [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies.
